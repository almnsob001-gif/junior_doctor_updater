# 🩺 Junior Doctor (JuniorMed Hub)

**Junior Doctor** is a comprehensive, offline-first medical reference application built for junior doctors, residents, and medical students. It provides quick access to clinical guidelines, emergency protocols, drug doses, and medical calculators in both **English and Arabic**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **📚 15+ Medical Specialties**: Internal medicine, pediatrics, OB/GYN, surgery, ENT, ophthalmology, orthopedics, urology, toxicology, and more.
- **🔄 Smart Auto-Updates**: Medical content updates seamlessly from GitHub without needing to reinstall the APK.
- **🌍 Bilingual Support**: Full English and Arabic support with RTL (Right-to-Left) text rendering.
- **🌙 Dark/Light Mode**: Eye-friendly themes for day and night shifts.
- **⭐ Favorites & Notes**: Save important topics and write personal clinical notes (stored locally and securely on your device).
- **🧮 Clinical Calculators**: Built-in tools including Estimated Date of Delivery (EDD), Gestational Age (GA), BMI, and more.
- **🔍 Advanced Search**: Global search by title and in-page search with text highlighting.
- **🔒 Privacy First**: No tracking, no ads, no accounts required. All personal data stays on your device.
